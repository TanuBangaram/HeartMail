About

A slower way to connect

In a world of instant messages, we forget the beauty of waiting.

HeartMail brings back the ritual of letter writing. One letter per week. Time to think, to feel, to express. No notifications, no pressure, no endless scrolling.

Just words on paper (digital paper, but still). And a little friend who grows with every letter you exchange.

How it works

Write, send, grow

01

Write your letter

Take your time. Choose your words. Add a drawing, a sticker, a personal touch. No rush.

02

Send it off

One letter per week. Address it to a friend, seal it with intention, and wait.

03

Watch it grow

Your shared friend grows with every letter. A little reminder of your connection.
